## Summary
This module explores the concept of the "Digital Twin" in robotics. It focuses on creating high-fidelity simulations where robots can be trained and tested without the risks and costs associated with physical hardware.

## Goals
- Define the role of Digital Twins in the Physical AI development lifecycle.
- Master robot modeling using URDF and SDF formats.
- Understand physics engines (ODE, Bullet) and their impact on simulation accuracy.
- Simulate complex sensors: LiDAR, Depth Cameras, and IMUs.
- Introduce Unity as a high-fidelity visualization and interaction tool.

## Requirements
### Functional
- Explain the transition from a static model (Module 1) to a dynamic simulation.
- Provide a setup guide for Gazebo Harmonic/Garden.
- Include tutorials on simulating world environments and adding sensors.
- Compare Gazebo (Physics-first) vs Unity (Visuals-first) for robotics.

### Non-Functional
- Tone: Technical, rigorous, and industrial.
- Structure: Educational with clear learning objectives.
- Formatting: High-quality code blocks and clear headings.

## Technical Details
- **Simulators**: Gazebo (Simulink/Classic) and Unity 2022 LTS.
- **Formats**: URDF (for ROS 2) and SDF (for Gazebo).
- **Physics**: ODE (Open Dynamics Engine) and Bullet.
- **Sensors**: Gazebo Sensor Plugins.
