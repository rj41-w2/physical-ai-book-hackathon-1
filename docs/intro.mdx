# Introduction: Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics Hackathon

Welcome to the **AI & Robotics Book**, a specialized textbook created for the **Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics Hackathon**. This project explores the convergence of artificial intelligence and physical actuators, bridging the gap between digital brains and physical bodies.

## Our Mission
The goal of this book is to provide a comprehensive, spec-driven guide to building embodied intelligence. We follow a structured learning path that takes students from the basic "nervous system" of a robot to advanced "Vision-Language-Action" (VLA) models.

## What You Will Learn
This textbook is divided into four core modules:
1. **Module 1: The Robotic Nervous System (ROS 2)** - Mastering the middleware of robotics.
2. **Module 2: The Digital Twin** - High-fidelity simulation and physics modeling.
3. **Module 3: The AI-Robot Brain (NVIDIA Isaac™)** - Hardware-accelerated perception and navigation.
4. **Module 4: Vision-Language-Action (VLA)** - Cognitive planning and LLM integration.

## Spec-Driven Development
This book itself is built using the **Spec-Kit Plus** methodology. Every chapter is first defined in a technical specification before being implemented. This ensures technical accuracy and architectural integrity across the entire project.
